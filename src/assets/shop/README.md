# Shop photos (About page gallery)

Put photos of the shop here — shop front, inside, counters, product displays — as
`.jpg`, `.png` or `.webp`. They appear on the About page (/about) automatically, in
file-name order, so name them `01-shop-front.jpg`, `02-inside.jpg`, … to choose the order.

- Landscape photos (4:3) look best; any size works — they are resized and converted to WebP at build time.
- Keep each original under ~5 MB.
- No faces of customers without their permission.

The gallery is hidden while this folder has no photos.
